    Import from SD card

    1. Remove the SD card from the AirSense 10 and put it in this computer.
    2. Choose the whole SD card folder (the one containing STR.edf and DATALOG).
    3. Check the preview, then press Save to Supabase.

    Files are read inside your browser. Only the summarised results (nightly statistics, per-minute curves, events, device settings) are uploaded. The raw .edf files are not.
